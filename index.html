How We Work

From Conversation to Production

A lean, four-phase cycle built for startups. No bloated discovery phases. No hand-off gaps.

01
Diagnose
A free, focused session to understand your data situation, existing stack, and where AI creates the most leverage. We define scope together.
02
Blueprint
We document requirements, propose an architecture, and produce a delivery plan — before a line of code is written. Two-week sprints from day one.
03
Build
Continuous delivery with CI/CD, weekly demos, and direct Slack access. You see progress every sprint, not just at the end.
04
Extend
Post-launch support, monitoring, and iterative improvement. We stay embedded as long as you need — scaling the team up or down with your roadmap.
